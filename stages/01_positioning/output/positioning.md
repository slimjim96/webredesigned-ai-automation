## What we sell (offer ladder)
| Offer | What it is | Role in the funnel |
|---|---|---|
| AI audit (advisory) | fixed-price assessment → prioritized roadmap | **entry offer**: low-risk first purchase, qualifies buyers |
| Workshops & team training | hands-on training for the owner and staff on Claude, Copilot, and similar tools, using their real tasks | follow-on from the audit roadmap |
| Custom skills & builds | custom AI skills tuned to the business, done-for-you automations, internal tools (e.g. directories, intake, quoting) | follow-on: builds what the audit prioritized |

**AI audit: $750** (recommended for the WNY market: under the "ask my partner" threshold, above "too cheap to be serious"). It includes:
- 90-minute discovery session, on-site in WNY or by video
- Review of current tools, workflows, and where time leaks (calls, scheduling, quoting, paperwork)
- Written roadmap: top 5 opportunities, each with estimated hours saved, rough cost, and difficulty
- 30-minute walkthrough call to go over the roadmap
- Audit fee **credited toward a workshop or build** booked within 30 days

Pricing on the site: **audit price only**. Workshops and builds say "quoted from your roadmap."
