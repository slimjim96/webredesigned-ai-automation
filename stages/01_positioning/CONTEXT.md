# 01_positioning — define audience, offers, angle

One job: decide who the site is for, what it sells, and why someone picks us over doing it themselves with an agent.

## Inputs
- Reference (every run): ../../docs/ai-automation.md (market thesis only)
- Reference (every run): ../../_shared/rules.md
- Working: owner interview answers, recorded directly into the output

Do NOT load: anything in later stages; brand or visual ideas (those belong in 02).

## Process
1. Interview the owner a few questions at a time; write answers into the output as they land.
2. Fill every section of the output; unresolved items stay as `TBD:` lines, not guesses.
3. Keep it under ~1 page. It is the brief every later stage reads.

## Outputs
- positioning.md → output/ (frontmatter `status: draft | approved`)

## Human check
Read the one-line positioning statement and the offer ladder as if you were an HVAC owner. If the answer isn't "that's me, and I know what to click", edit before approving.
