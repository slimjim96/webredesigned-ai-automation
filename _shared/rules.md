# Rules that never bend

1. **No borrowed claims.** Nothing from `docs/` appears on the site as our fact: no revenue figures, guarantees, benchmark scores, or student counts. Ideas inform positioning; proof must be our own.
2. **No invented proof.** Testimonials, logos, case studies, and numbers are real or absent. Placeholders are marked `[[PROOF NEEDED]]` and block launch.
3. **Plain language for SMB owners.** Outcomes (hours saved, leads answered) before tooling (agents, MCP, APIs). Jargon only where the buyer already uses it.
4. **One primary action.** Every page drives toward the single primary CTA defined in `stages/01_positioning/output/positioning.md`.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard navigable, works at 360px width.
