Name

Techne is Greek for craft or skill; hora is the hour. Two words, both capitalized. Never “TechneHora”, “Techne-Hora”, or “Techne Hora AI” in the logo.

Logo set

A navy-rimmed clock at ten o’clock, the last two hours tinted: the hours given back. “Techne” is set in Plex Serif, with H·O·R·A under it in Plex Mono, spaced like hour marks the way a watch prints small text on its dial. The whole name stays readable, and Hora stays quiet.

Rules

  • Minimum size: lockups at least 48px / 16mm tall, so H·O·R·A reads. Smaller than that, use the mark alone; below 24px, the favicon.
  • Clear space: the rim’s width (about 14% of the mark’s height) on every side.
  • Don’t: recolor or rotate the mark, set HORA heavier or in color, add the rewind arc to the static logo, or put the full-color mark on a busy photo.

Motion

The ember rewind arc is the clearest picture of “time back”, so it is kept for moments that move, never inside the logo.

Color

Every text pair passes WCAG 2.2 AA. Ember is for graphics on navy only, never for text on paper.
