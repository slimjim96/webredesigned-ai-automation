## FAQ: The call
- **SectionHeading H2:** The free call

1. **What happens on the free call?**
   You tell us how your week runs and where it hurts. We ask questions, tell you whether we think we can help, and if so, explain how the audit works. No slides, no pressure.
2. **Is it really free?**
   Yes. 20 minutes, no card, no catch. If we can't help, you've lost 20 minutes and maybe gained an idea.
3. **Phone or video?**
   Whichever you like. The calendar invite has both.

## FAQ: The audit
- **SectionHeading H2:** The audit

1. **What do I get for $750?**
   A 90-minute session, on-site in Western NY or by video, then a written roadmap of your top 5 fixes with estimated hours saved, rough cost and difficulty, and a 30-minute walkthrough.
2. **What if you don't find anything worth fixing?**
   Then the roadmap says so, and tells you what isn't worth automating yet. That's worth knowing before you spend money on software. [[OWNER: refund or partial credit policy, same as `audit.md`]]
3. **Do I have to hire you afterward?**
   No. The roadmap is yours. If you book training or a build within 30 days, the $750 is credited toward it.

## FAQ: Training
- **SectionHeading H2:** Training

1. **Which tools do you train on?**
   Mostly Claude and Microsoft Copilot, plus whatever your business already uses.
2. **My team isn't techy. Will they keep up?**
   That's who we design it for. If someone can write a text message, they can do this.

## FAQ: Cost & data
- **SectionHeading H2:** Cost and your data

1. **Why don't you list prices for training and builds?**
   Because they depend on your team and your roadmap. A price before we know either would just be a guess. The audit is $750, fixed.
2. **Do I have to buy new software?**
   Not to start. The roadmap begins with the tools you already pay for. If something new would pay for itself, it says what, roughly what it costs, and why.
3. **What happens to my data?**

4. **Will AI replace my staff?**
