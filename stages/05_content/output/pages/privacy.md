## SectionHeading + list
- **H2:** What we collect
- **List:**
  - **When you book a call:** your name, email, phone number if you give it, and the time you picked. The booking tool ([[BUILD: Cal.com, if chosen]]) stores this so the appointment works.
  - **When you send the form:** what you typed in it. It comes to us by email.
  - **When you visit:** a count of page views and where visitors came from, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you. [[BUILD: Plausible or Cloudflare Web Analytics, whichever is chosen]]
  - **When you call or email:** what you tell us, like any business.

## SectionHeading + list
- **H2:** What we don't do
- **List:**
  - We don't sell your information, rent it, or trade it.
  - We don't use advertising trackers or cookies that follow you around the internet.
  - We don't add you to a mailing list. We don't have one.
