## SectionHeading + list
- **H2:** What a session covers
- **Lede:** No slideshow about the future of work. We use the tasks on your desk this week.
- **List:**
  - **Writing:** estimates, customer emails, job write-ups and the reply to that one-star review
  - **Organizing:** turning notes, photos and voice memos into something you can send
  - **Scheduling:** shifts, routes and appointment changes with less back-and-forth
  - **Finding things:** asking your own documents, price lists and manuals a question instead of digging
  - **Knowing the limits:** what to double-check, what never to paste in, and when to just pick up the phone

## SectionHeading + list
- **H2:** Formats
- **Lede:** Pick what fits your team. The roadmap usually points to one.
- **List:**
  - **Half-day on-site session** for the owner and the people who run the office or the floor
  - **Owner 1-on-1:** one or two sessions with just you, in person or by video
  - **Short team sessions** of 60 to 90 minutes, one task each, for teams that can't all be in one room
  - **Group classes:** a few local businesses in one room, learning the basics together

## Steps
- **SectionHeading H2:** How training works

1. **Pick the tasks.** From your roadmap, we choose the 3 to 5 jobs that eat the most time.
2. **Work through them together.** Hands-on, with your files and your tools. Everyone leaves having done it, not just watched it.
3. **Keep it.** Each person gets a one-page cheat sheet for their tasks, and we check in once the habits have had time to form.

## FAQ
- **SectionHeading H2:** Questions about training

1. **Which tools do you train on?**
   Mostly Claude and Microsoft Copilot, plus whatever your business already uses. If you're paying for it, it should be pulling its weight.
2. **My team isn't techy. Will they keep up?**
   That's who we design it for. If someone can write a text message, they can do this. We go at the pace of the room.
3. **Can we do it without the audit?**
   Talk to us on the free call. If you already know exactly what you need, we can skip ahead. Usually the audit saves you from training on the wrong thing.
4. **Is our information safe in these tools?**
   Part of every session is what not to paste in, and which settings to check. When we work in your tools, we look with you, on your screen, and don't take copies of your data.
