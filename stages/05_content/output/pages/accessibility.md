# Accessibility

## Hero (no art)
- **H1:** This site should work for everyone.
- **Lede:** If something on it doesn't work for you, that's our problem to fix, and we want to hear about it.

## SectionHeading + list
- **H2:** What we aim for
- **List:**
  - **The standard:** WCAG 2.2 level AA, the common benchmark for accessible websites.
  - **Readable:** text and buttons meet AA color contrast in both light and dark mode.
  - **Keyboard-friendly:** everything works without a mouse, with a visible focus outline.
  - **Phone-friendly:** every page works on a screen 360 pixels wide, with buttons big enough for a thumb.
  - **Screen readers:** pages use real headings, labels and landmarks so they make sense out loud.
  - **Calm:** nothing moves on its own, except a small loading icon, which stops if your device asks for reduced motion.
