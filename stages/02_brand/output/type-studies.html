Rules every pairing follows

  • Body text 16px or larger, line height about 1.5 (owners read on phones in trucks)
  • Lines 45 to 75 characters wide
  • Two families at most, plus a mono only if it earns its place
  • Variable fonts where possible: one file covers every weight (faster pages, more flexibility)
  • No weights under 400 below 20px
  • Tabular figures for prices and hours
  • font-display: swap and a matched fallback stack, so text shows instantly
  • Latin Extended coverage for names and accents. Most of these faces have no Greek letters, so if the site ever shows τέχνη ὥρα, set it in a face that does (Source Sans 3, IBM Plex Sans)
  • Open license (OFL): free for the site, print, vans, and the logo
Wordmarks in the mockups are live text in the selected heading face. The final logo gets re-outlined in whichever face wins. Contact details on the card are placeholders.
