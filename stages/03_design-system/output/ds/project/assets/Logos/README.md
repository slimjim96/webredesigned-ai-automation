The Techne Hora clock mark and lockups from `stages/02_brand/output/logo/`. The lockups' lettering is Fraunces and will be replaced by the redesign in `logo-design-brief.md`; the mark stays.

- `technehora-mark.svg`: full color with subtle shading. Social avatars, the hero, app icons. Works on `bg` and `inverse`.
- `technehora-mark-flat.svg`: no gradients. Print and signage.
- `technehora-mark-mono-navy.svg`: one ink, navy `#1B2A4A`. Embroidery, stamps, fax.
- `technehora-mark-mono-white.svg`: one ink, white `#FFFFFF`, for navy or photo backgrounds.
- `favicon.svg`: simplified mark for 16–24px.
- `technehora-lockup-horizontal-light.svg` / `-dark.svg`: mark plus name on one line. Light for `bg`, dark for `inverse`.
- `technehora-lockup-stacked-light.svg`: mark over name. Shirts, signs, title slides.
- `technehora-lockup-compact-light.svg`: name on two lines beside the mark. Van doors, cards.
