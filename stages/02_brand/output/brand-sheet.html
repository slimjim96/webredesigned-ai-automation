Logo set

A cream clock face lifted off a navy disc, hands at 10 and 12, the last two hours tinted, and a brick arrow winding the dial back. Wordmark is Fraunces Bold, outlined. Keep clear space equal to the clock-face radius; below 24px use the favicon.
