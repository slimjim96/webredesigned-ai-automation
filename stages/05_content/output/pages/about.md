## SectionHeading + text
- **H2:** Why the name
- **Paragraphs:**
  - *Techne* is the Greek word for craft or skill. *Hora* means the hour. We craft AI tools that give your hours back.
  - It's said **TEK-nee OR-ah**, with a soft H. If you say it some other way, we'll still answer the phone.

## SectionHeading + list
- **H2:** How we work
- **List:**
  - **Plan before we spend.** The audit comes first, so your money goes to the fixes that save the most time.
  - **Teach, don't just install.** A tool your team doesn't understand is a tool they'll stop using. Training is part of the job.
  - **Plain words.** If we can't explain it without jargon, we don't understand it well enough yet.
  - **Honest answers.** If AI won't help with something, we'll tell you, even if it means less work for us.
  - **Your business, your tools.** What we build is yours, set up so you can keep running it.
