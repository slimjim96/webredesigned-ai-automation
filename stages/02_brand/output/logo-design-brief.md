# Logo design brief: Techne Hora

A prompt pack for taking the logo further with other tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Recraft, Ideogram, Midjourney, or a human designer). Paste **Part 1** as the full brief, then add **Part 2** (for tools that write SVG code) or **Part 3** (for image generators). ## Part 1: the brief (paste this first)

You are designing the logo system for **Techne Hora**, a one-person AI consultancy in Rochester, New York.

**What the business does.** It helps owners of local businesses (HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors first, then restaurants) get hours back. It finds where their time leaks (missed calls, scheduling, quoting, paperwork), trains their staff to use AI tools like Claude and Copilot well, and builds small custom tools around how that business actually works. The entry offer is a $750 audit.

**Who has to like it.** A 50-year-old HVAC owner who has never hired a consultant and distrusts "tech bros." The logo must look trustworthy, practical, and well made, like a good tool brand or an established local firm, not like a startup or an AI company. It will appear on a van door, an embroidered work shirt, a business card, a website header, a social avatar, and a 16px browser tab.

**The name.**
- *Techne* (Greek τέχνη) means craft, skill, the art of making. *Hora* (ὥρα) means the hour.
- Together: the craft of the hour, skilled work that gives your time back.
- Said **TEK-nee OR-ah**, with a soft, almost silent H. The name is meant to sound distinctive and a little rhythmic; the design should embrace that, not hide it.
- Always two words, both capitalized: "Techne Hora". Never "TechneHora", "Techne-Hora", or with "AI" added.
- Tagline (optional, under the name): "The craft of getting your hours back."
- Descriptor (optional, small): "AI help for local businesses"

**The existing mark (keep its idea, you may redraw it).** A wall-clock dial seen face on:
- A navy disc, with a cream clock face set inside it.
- Twelve tick marks; the 12, 3, 6, and 9 ticks are heavier.
- Hands at roughly 10:00. The minute hand is navy and points straight up to 12; the hour hand is brick red and points to 10.
- The wedge between 10 and 12 is tinted a pale blush: "the last two hours."
- A tapered coral arrow wraps around the outside of the dial **counter-clockwise**, from about 2 o'clock, over the top, down to about 9 o'clock. It is thin at the start, thick at the arrowhead. Meaning: **time wound back.**
- A small navy hub with a cream center pin.

**What I want now.** The current logo is the clock sitting next to the name set in plain type. I want a **more creative system where the name and the clock work together**: the name is part of the idea, not a caption beside it. Explore ideas like these (and your own):
1. **The O in "Hora" becomes the clock** (hands set at 10, the counter-clockwise arrow around it). The rest of the word is set in the brand type.
2. **The H as the clock's hands**: the crossbar of the H, or a TH ligature, forms hands at 10:00 inside a dial.
3. **The arrow carries the name**: the counter-clockwise rewind arrow sweeps under or around "Techne Hora," with the dial as its anchor.
4. **A TH monogram dial**: T and H set inside the clock face, where the T's stem is the minute hand. Good for avatars, embroidery, and the favicon.
5. **Badge / seal**: "TECHNE HORA" set on a circle around the dial like a watchmaker's or workshop stamp, with "ROCHESTER, NY" or "EST. 2026" on the bottom arc. Good for shirts and stickers.
6. **A quiet Greek nod**: a subtle eta (η) or omega (ω) detail hidden in a letterform. Keep it subtle, so it doesn't read as a fraternity.

**Color (exact values; use no others).**
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink navy | `#1B2A4A` | main color: lettering, dial disc |
| Paper | `#FAF7F0` | cream clock face, light backgrounds |
| Brick | `#A63D2A` | hour hand, one accent |
| Ember | `#E07A5F` | the rewind arrow only (graphic, never small text) |
| Blush | `#F3D3C8` | the tinted two-hour wedge |
| Sage | `#4E7060` | optional secondary accent |
| Graphite | `#5C6170` | tagline and descriptor text |
| Deep navy | `#111B31` | dark backgrounds |

**Type.** The brand type is **IBM Plex** (free, open license):
- **IBM Plex Serif** SemiBold or Bold for the name.
- **IBM Plex Sans** for the tagline and descriptor.
- **IBM Plex Mono** for small "engineered" details (EST. 2026, coordinates, the numerals on a badge).

You may customize letterforms (ligatures, trimmed serifs, a clock-shaped O), but the result should still read as Plex's precise, engineered-but-human character. Letter spacing on the wordmark should be slightly open. Use title case for the wordmark; all caps is allowed only on badge rings.

**Feel: yes.** A crafted tool, precise, warm, established, a workshop more than an office, mechanical watch, honest, local.
**Feel: no.** A robot, a brain, a circuit board, neural nodes, sparkles or AI stars, a lightbulb, glowing gradients, neon, 3D chrome, a hipster-barbershop look, a clip-art clock, Greek columns, a laurel wreath, togas.

**Hard requirements.**
- Works in **one color** (navy only, and white only) with no loss of meaning: vinyl cut, embroidery, stamps, photocopies.
- The mark alone must be recognizable at **16px** (a simplified favicon version is fine) and at **24px**.
- No thin lines that disappear when embroidered: minimum stroke about 1/40 of the mark's width at full size.
- No gradients in the primary version. One optional "full color" version may use a subtle shadow or depth.
- Text in the logo must be real, correctly spelled letters: **Techne Hora**. Check the spelling twice.
- Readable on cream `#FAF7F0` and on navy `#1B2A4A`. Text colors must meet WCAG AA contrast.
- All final files are **vector (SVG)**, with text **converted to outlines**, plus PNG exports.

**Deliverables (for the direction I pick).**
| Variation | Light-bg version | Dark-bg version |
|---|---|---|
| Primary horizontal lockup (mark + name, one line) | yes | yes |
| Stacked lockup (mark centered above name) | yes | yes |
| Compact lockup (name on two lines beside the mark) | yes | yes |
| Integrated wordmark (the name with the clock built in) | yes | yes |
| Monogram / mark alone | yes | yes |
| Badge / seal | yes | yes |
| One-color navy and one-color white of each | yes | |
| Favicon (simplified mark, 16/32/48px) | yes | |

PNG exports: 512 and 1024px for the mark; 180px apple-touch icon on a cream background; lockups at 2× website size, on transparent backgrounds.

**How to work.** First show **4 to 6 distinct concepts** as quick black-and-white sketches, each with a one-line rationale. Wait for me to pick one or two. Then refine those in color, and only then build the full variation set.

## Part 3: short prompts for image generators

Image models misspell text and output raster images. Use them for **ideas**, not final files. **Recraft** (set it to vector / "Vector art" style) and **Ideogram** handle text best. Once you pick a direction, rebuild it as an SVG with a Part 2 tool or a designer, or redraw it in Inkscape, Figma, or Illustrator.

Attach the reference PNG, then run one prompt at a time:

1. **Clock in the O**
   > Minimal flat vector wordmark "Techne Hora", elegant engineered serif lettering like IBM Plex Serif, the letter O in "Hora" is a small clock face with hands at ten o'clock and a coral arrow curving counter-clockwise around it, navy #1B2A4A lettering on cream #FAF7F0 background, brick red #A63D2A hour hand, no gradients, crisp, professional, generous whitespace, logo design

2. **TH monogram dial**
   > Flat vector monogram logo, letters T and H combined inside a round clock dial, the T stem doubles as the minute hand, a brick red hour hand points to ten, twelve tick marks, a tapered coral #E07A5F arrow wraps counter-clockwise around the outside, navy #1B2A4A and cream #FAF7F0 only, bold simple shapes that work as embroidery and as a tiny app icon, no gradients, no text besides T and H

3. **Workshop seal**
   > Circular badge logo, flat vector, a clock dial with hands at ten o'clock in the center, "TECHNE HORA" in spaced capital letters around the top arc, "ROCHESTER, NY" around the bottom arc, thin double ring border, navy #1B2A4A on cream #FAF7F0 with one coral #E07A5F counter-clockwise arrow accent, watchmaker's workshop stamp style, clean, no distress texture, no gradients

4. **Rewind arrow under the name**
   > Horizontal logo lockup, flat vector, a small navy round clock dial on the left with hands at ten o'clock, a tapered coral #E07A5F arrow begins at the dial and sweeps counter-clockwise underneath the wordmark "Techne Hora" set in a refined engineered serif, navy #1B2A4A text on cream background, minimal, professional consultancy brand, no gradients

Negative prompt (where supported): `robot, brain, circuit, neural network, sparkles, glow, neon, 3D, chrome, gradient, lightbulb, greek column, laurel, toga, clip art, photorealistic, misspelled text, extra letters`

## Review checklist (when designs come back)
- [ ] The spelling is exactly "Techne Hora" (check the e's and the order of h and n)
- [ ] It still reads as a clock, with time going back (the arrow runs counter-clockwise)
- [ ] One-color navy and one-color white versions work
- [ ] The mark is recognizable at 24px; there's a favicon for 16px
- [ ] Only brand colors are used; text passes AA on cream and on navy
- [ ] Files are SVG with outlined text, plus PNGs
- [ ] None of the "Feel: no" items appear
- [ ] Font licenses allow logo use (IBM Plex is OFL: yes)
